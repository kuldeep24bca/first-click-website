About

Designing for the Web Since Class 8

I'm Kuldeep Gehlot — a Digital Marketer, UI/UX & Web Designer, and Entrepreneur.

I've worked with 50+ clients across 20+ different niches and generated ₹30+ Lakhs in revenue through digital marketing and digital projects. Most of that work looks like the previews above: websites, landing pages and funnels where design and conversion are the same conversation.

Alongside client work, I share what I've learned through workshops and educational content — helping students apply digital skills to real projects.

  • Design that's judged on what it converts, not just how it looks
  • Built mobile-first — most of this traffic arrives on a phone
  • From first wireframe to live site, handled end to end
  • Worked across e-commerce, SaaS, education, hospitality & coaching
